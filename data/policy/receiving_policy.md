# Receiving and Payment-Hold Policy

**FourthMatch Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd.**
**Document No. FM-POL-PROC-001**
**Version 1.0**
**Effective Date: 2026-04-01**

## 1 Purpose

**1.1** This Policy establishes the standard governing receipt, verification, documentation and payment control for materials received at plant stores against approved Purchase Orders.

**1.2** This Policy governs all receipts at plant stores covering raw materials, packing materials, cartons, bagged material and steel coils, together with all associated invoices, debit notes and payment holds.

**1.3** This Policy ensures that stock records reflect only verified and acceptable material, and that payment is released only for such material in accordance with agreed terms and approvals.

**1.4** All personnel engaged in indenting, purchasing, stores receipt, quality inspection, and finance booking and payment shall comply with this Policy.

## 2 Definitions

**2.1** Purchase Order means the approved order placed on a supplier that states the material, quantity, price and delivery terms.

**2.2** For the purposes of this policy, Goods Receipt Note means the record maintained by stores confirming receipt of material, verification of quantity, and any damage or shortage observed.

**2.3** Damage is defined as breakage, leakage, rust, deformation, contamination, wetting, or any condition rendering material unfit for intended use, as confirmed by Quality Assurance.

**2.4** A Debit Note is defined as a written claim issued to a supplier to recover value in respect of damage, shortage, rejection, or price correction.

**2.5** Hold refers to a formally documented status that prevents the booking or payment of an invoice or part of such invoice until the stated conditions are cleared.

**2.6** For purposes of this Policy, Evidence means photographs, video recordings, weighment slips, count sheets, inspection reports and system tracking logs linked to a Goods Receipt Note.

**2.7** For the purposes of this policy, the term Supplier denotes the party identified in the Purchase Order as the seller and issuer of the invoice.

**2.8** The term Finance Controller refers to the head of plant finance who is responsible for booking, hold control and payment release in conjunction with Purchase as described herein.

## 3 Roles and sign-off

**3.1** The Storekeeper shall supervise unloading operations, verify count and weighment, truthfully record the findings, and sign the Goods Receipt Note to confirm receipt as recorded.

**3.2** Where shortage, excess, or damage is observed, Quality Assurance shall inspect the consignment and shall co-sign the Goods Receipt Note and the damage record to confirm findings and disposition pending details.

**3.3** The Stores Manager shall approve Goods Receipt Notes and invoices with invoice value up to ₹1,00,000.

**3.4** The Purchase Manager shall approve Goods Receipt Notes and invoices where the invoice value is up to ₹5,00,000.

**3.5** The Plant Head shall approve Goods Receipt Notes and invoices with an invoice value above ₹5,00,000.

**3.6** Payment holds imposed under this Policy shall be released by the Purchase Manager and the Finance Controller acting jointly, after due verification and recording of clearance.

**3.7** Any individual with a conflict of interest is prohibited from approving any receipt, invoice, or hold release, and all such matters must be escalated to a higher authority for determination.

**3.8** Stores and Finance shall ensure that personnel who handle receipts, inspection and payment are trained in this Policy and in correct documentation.

## 4 Quantity verification

**4.1** The quantity shall be verified at the time of unloading against the Purchase Order and the supplier challan, employing count for countable units and weighment where applicable, and combined methods where required by Purchase.

**4.2** Quantity tolerance for acceptance without escalation shall be 0.0% for cartons, 2.0% for bagged material, and 0.0% for steel coils, and any shortage or excess beyond the applicable tolerance shall be documented and shall require review and approval in accordance with sign-off limits before closure of the Goods Receipt Note.

**4.3** Weighment slips, count sheets, and supporting photographs of the vehicle, unloading, stacking, and any shortage or excess shall be attached to the Goods Receipt Note.

**4.4** Excess quantity that is neither required nor approved shall be segregated within a marked hold area, shall be kept out of usable stock, and shall be returned to the supplier or regularised through a Purchase Order amendment prior to use.

**4.5** Any shortfall in quantity relative to the Purchase Order shall be documented as a short receipt, with the Goods Receipt Note indicating solely the quantity received in acceptable condition.

**4.6** The Goods Receipt Note shall be prepared during the same shift as unloading wherever feasible and shall specify vehicle details, challan details, and the method of verification for purposes of traceability.

## 5 Invoice verification

**5.1** The invoice price must be matched against the Purchase Order price; a variation of up to 2.0% may be accepted subject to approval in accordance with sign-off limits, whereas any variation exceeding the stated tolerance must be held pending Purchase clarification and correction of the Purchase Order price prior to booking.

**5.2** Invoiced quantity shall be matched to the Purchase Order quantity and the Goods Receipt Note quantity, with tolerance for invoiced quantity above Purchase Order quantity set at 0.0%, and any invoiced quantity above Purchase Order quantity shall be held and shall not be paid until the Purchase Order is duly amended and approved.

**5.3** Any invoice bearing the same supplier and supplier invoice number as an invoice already booked shall be treated as a duplicate invoice, shall not be booked again, and payment thereon shall be held at 100.0% of its value until the supplier proves that it is not a second presentation of the same supply.

**5.4** Supplier name, tax particulars, Purchase Order reference and Goods Receipt Note reference shall be verified prior to booking, and any invoice bearing a material mismatch in these particulars shall be held until corrected by the supplier through a compliant document.

**5.5** Credit notes received from suppliers in respect of shortage, damage, or price correction shall be linked to the corresponding invoice and debit note and shall be adjusted prior to release of payment.

## 6 Damage and debit notes

**6.1** Damaged units identified at receipt or during inspection shall be recorded separately and shall not be taken into usable stock, the payable quantity for such damaged units shall be 0, and debit for such damaged units shall be raised at 100.0% of the value of such units at the Purchase Order unit price.

**6.2** Quality Assurance shall complete inspection and issue a written disposition for damaged, short, or rejected material within 5 working days of recording in the Goods Receipt Note, specifying acceptance, rejection, segregation, return, or disposal as applicable.

**6.3** The Purchase department shall issue the debit note to the supplier within 7 working days of the Goods Receipt Note, and the Finance department shall adjust such debit note against the supplier account prior to release of the related payment.

**6.4** Damaged and rejected material awaiting return or disposal shall be kept segregated in store custody with clear identification and shall be dispatched or disposed only following documented authorisation.

**6.5** In cases where damage appears to have occurred during transit, the Purchase function shall file a claim with the transporter in addition to raising a debit against the supplier, and such claim shall be supported by photographic and video evidence.

## 7 Payment and payment holds

**7.1** Standard payment terms shall be 30 days from the booking of a complete, matched and approved invoice with Goods Receipt Note, subject to holds under this Policy, and invoices that are incomplete, mismatched or under hold shall not be treated as due until defects are cured and approval is complete.

**7.2** Finance shall place payment on hold where quantity, price, damage, duplicate invoice or evidence requirements are not met, and shall intimate Purchase and the supplier within 3 working days of identification of the hold, stating reasons for the hold and documents required for release.

**7.3** A hold shall be released jointly by the Purchase Manager and the Finance Controller only after the underlying mismatch is corrected, damage is settled through debit note, or required approval and evidence are completed and duly recorded.

**7.4** Release may be permitted for the undisputed and accepted portion of an invoice that is fully matched, documented and approved, while the disputed portion shall remain on hold until final resolution and documented closure.

**7.5** Payment shall not be released for held invoices through advances or adjustments that bypass hold controls, and any exception shall require written justification with joint approval and full audit trail.

## 8 Evidence

**8.1** Unloading, counting, weighment and inspection of consignments shall be supported by continuous video evidence showing vehicle, material, handling and recording activity, and such recordings shall be linked to the Goods Receipt Note and preserved for audit and review, and where dock video exists, the counted units shall be the unique object tracks counted from that video.

**8.2** Where automated video tracking is used to support quantity or damage verification, only tracks with confidence at or above 0.80 shall be relied upon as system evidence, and settings and logs for such tracking shall be retained with the Goods Receipt Note.

**8.3** If video evidence is missing, incomplete, or assessed below the confidence stated in this Policy, the matter shall be routed to human review with manual recount or reweighment and independent verification, and payment for the affected portion shall remain on hold until such human review is complete and approved, and weak evidence shall never serve as a basis for approval.

**8.4** A Goods Receipt Note signed by the storekeeper serves as a receiving record and shall not constitute definitive proof when contradicted by video evidence; where the video record and the Goods Receipt Note are in disagreement, the count shown by the video shall prevail for payment purposes and the Goods Receipt Note shall be corrected by means of a signed amendment.

**8.5** Video evidence storage and retrieval shall preserve integrity and prevent alteration, with access restricted to authorised personnel and viewing logged for audit, and an evidence pack comprising documents, counts, the decision and the clauses relied on shall be generated for every held or reviewed delivery and hash-sealed so that it can be re-verified later.

## 9 Records and review

**9.1** Purchase Orders, Goods Receipt Notes, invoices, debit notes, inspection and disposition records, hold and release records, and video evidence and tracking logs shall be preserved and maintained in retrievable form for audit for 7 years.

**9.2** Stores, Purchase, Quality Assurance and Finance shall review holds, debit notes and evidence gaps from time to time, and shall initiate corrective action with suppliers, transporters and internal teams to prevent recurrence.

**9.3** This Policy shall be subject to review from time to time to confirm continued suitability, and amendments shall be approved and communicated before implementation, with training provided to concerned personnel. Tolerances may be changed only by a new numbered version of this document.

**9.4** Any non-compliance with this Policy shall be reported through the defined escalation path and shall be addressed through corrective action, retraining, or disciplinary measures as applicable under Company rules.
